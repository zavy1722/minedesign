# State of the art 2025-2026 in 3D face reconstruction

> From 2024 to 2026 the field split into two tracks. The **geometry track** moved from DECA-style coefficient regression to foundation-feature dense prediction plus optimisation (Pixel3DMM) and neural-renderer self-supervision (SMIRK → TEASER → SHeaP); NoW, the neutral-shape benchmark, is near saturation at roughly 0.75–0.9 mm median. The **appearance track** made FLAME-bound 3D Gaussians the default avatar and handles few-shot input with feed-forward models (GAGAvatar, LAM, Avat3r) or diffusion that invents missing views (CAP4D, FaceLift, GAF); these optimise photorealism, not millimetres, while geometry foundation models (DUSt3R/MASt3R, VGGT) now initialise cameras from a few uncalibrated photos. For a sculpt-ready likeness the best open recipe is still a multi-view perspective landmark-plus-silhouette FLAME or template fit, initialised by MICA, refined with dense face-specific cues, with generated views kept out of the identity solve. No harvest or draft existed: this is author synthesis (reliable to about mid-2025) plus pointers to 2026 papers harvested in sibling notes, and nearly every strong component is research-licensed.

## Key facts

**Scope and horizon**
- Recall is dependable through CVPR/SIGGRAPH 2025 and thins after mid-2025; the 2026 CVPR, SIGGRAPH and ECCV programmes were not reviewed. 2026 papers here come from sibling notes, marked "(lib: file)", which hold their URLs. 🧠

**Shift 1 — single-image geometry: from regression to dense prediction plus optimisation**
- **Pixel3DMM** (Giebenhain et al., 2025). Fine-tuned DINO-family ViTs predict per-pixel surface normals and FLAME UV coordinates; FLAME is then *optimised* against them. It reports over 15 % better geometry than the strongest baselines on posed expressions, and adds a NeRSemble-based benchmark that scores both neutral and posed faces. Code is public [3]; accepted as an ICLR 2026 poster [2] (venue corrected in verification); code and models are under CC BY-NC 4.0 per the repository README [3] (non-commercial; FLAME terms also apply). 📄
- **Dense correspondences replace sparse landmarks.** Dense UV-to-image flow tracking (CVPR 2024) and RealDenseFace (2026, real-time) fit FLAME to thousands of correspondences instead of 68 points (lib: multi-view-fitting.md). 🧠
- **Self-supervision through a neural renderer.**
  - SMIRK (CVPR 2024) renders the predicted geometry through a neural renderer.
  - TEASER (ICLR 2025) adds multi-scale tokens; its claim to beat SMIRK is unverified (lib: single-image-regression.md).
  - SHeaP (Schoneveld et al., 2025) trains a FLAME regressor with a photometric loss through 2D Gaussians rigged to the predicted mesh, and reports gains on NoW and on posed expressions.

  All three target expression and the shape of the face as seen in the image, not metric identity. 🧠
- **LRMs constrained to a 3DMM.** KaoLRM (2026) repurposes a pretrained large reconstruction model (LRM) to output FLAME parameters. A 2026 profile-specific regressor targets lateral photos, where MICA scores 0.94 / 1.16 mm median / mean (lib: single-image-regression.md). 🧠
- **3DDFA-V3** (CVPR 2024, BFM) uses face-part segmentation as a geometric loss and is strong on REALY's region-wise errors. 🧠
- **MICA (ECCV 2022) is still the default identity initialiser.** A 2026 multi-view Gaussian paper predicts identity with MICA and fits with the Metrical Tracker (lib: multi-view-fitting.md). 🧠
- **NoW is saturating.** The best leaderboard entries sit around 0.76–0.9 mm median [4] and 0.95–1.1 mm mean, against 1.53 mm for a scale-fitted *average* face. New methods often differ by under 0.1 mm, which is comparable to alignment noise, and metrics are mixed (Chamfer vs scan-to-mesh). ↔️

**Shift 2 — Gaussian avatars became the default appearance model**
- **GaussianAvatars** (Qian et al., CVPR 2024) binds 3D Gaussians to FLAME triangles and trains on 16-camera NeRSemble video. Its tracker, VHAP, is reused by GAF (CVPR 2025) (lib: multi-view-fitting.md). 🧠
- **Successors:** NPGA (SIGGRAPH Asia 2024), URAvatar (Meta, SIGGRAPH Asia 2024; relightable avatar from a phone scan via a universal prior), and Gaussian eigen-model work (GEM, 2025) that compresses avatars back into linear bases. 🧠
- **No surface.** These avatars render photoreal views in real time but have no watertight surface: geometry is the tracked FLAME mesh plus splats. Papers report PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS and identity cosine, rarely millimetres. 🧠

**Shift 3 — few-shot input via learned priors and generated views**
- **Feed-forward animatable heads.** GAGAvatar (NeurIPS 2024, one image), LAM (SIGGRAPH 2025: one image to a FLAME-driven Gaussian head in one pass, WebGL-renderable) and Avat3r (2025: a few posed images; uses DUSt3R position maps and Sapiens features; trained on multi-view studio data). Inference takes seconds. 🧠
- **Generative fill-in.**
  - CAP4D (CVPR 2025): morphable multi-view diffusion from 1 to about 100 references, then a 4D Gaussian fit.
  - FaceLift (2025, reportedly ICCV): multi-view diffusion, then GS-LRM; a 360° head from one photo. DiffPortrait360 (CVPR 2025) is similar.
  - Arc2Avatar (CVPR 2025): score distillation with identity-conditioned Arc2Face (ECCV 2024). SynShot (CVPR 2025): few-shot inversion of a synthetic-data prior. GAF (CVPR 2025): diffusion-regularised monocular-video avatars.

  🧠
- **Unseen regions are sampled, not measured.** The back of the head, ears and far profile come from the prior; identity-conditioned diffusion preserves *recognisability*, not 3D shape. ↔️
- **Generic image-to-3D.** TRELLIS (CVPR 2025), Hunyuan3D 2.x (2025) and Meta's SAM 3D (reported November 2025) produce full textured meshes that are plausible but averaged and non-metric (lib: community/ai-assisted-sculpting.md). ↔️

**Shift 4 — cameras and geometry from uncalibrated photos**
- **Feed-forward geometry.**
  - DUSt3R (CVPR 2024) and MASt3R (ECCV 2024) regress point maps for image pairs.
  - VGGT (CVPR 2025 best paper) regresses cameras, depth and point maps for one to hundreds of images in one pass, at about a billion parameters.
  - π³ and MapAnything (2025) are in the same family, and Depth Anything 3 (November 2025) adds any-view depth plus rays (lib: monocular-depth-normals.md).

  🧠
- **Marginalized Bundle Adjustment** (2026) initialises intrinsics from a DUSt3R point map (lib: camera-pose-solving.md). 🧠
- **Not validated for faces.** None is shown to be millimetre-accurate on face-only photo sets (small, low-texture, non-rigid between shots). ↔️

**Shift 5 — human foundation models and studio data**
- **Sapiens** (ECCV 2024) predicts 308 keypoints (mostly facial), segmentation that includes hair, lips, teeth and glasses, and normals. **DAViD** (2025) is trained on synthetic data (lib: monocular-depth-normals.md). 🧠
- **Multi-view head datasets now drive priors:** NeRSemble, Ava-256, RenderMe-360 and FaceScape (Table 5). Their populations skew young and toward East Asian or European adults. 🧠

**Licences**
- **Research-only is the norm.**
  - FLAME 2020 and BFM are non-commercial.
  - InsightFace/ArcFace weights, which MICA and Arc2Face depend on, are non-commercial.
  - DUSt3R and MASt3R are CC BY-NC-SA.
  - VGGT shipped non-commercial weights first; a separate commercial-use checkpoint (VGGT-1B-Commercial, commercial use except military applications, gated by an application form) was released later [5][6].
  - The permissive pieces are MediaPipe (Apache-2.0), TRELLIS (MIT) and "FLAME 2023 Open" (CC BY 4.0, confirmed on the official FLAME licence page [1]; FLAME 2017/2019/2020/2023 and the Blender add-on stay non-commercial, and the texture model is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). 📄

  ↔️

## Applying this to photo→3D face sculpting in Blender

1. **Choose the deliverable first.** A sculpt-ready likeness needs clean topology and measured depth, so Gaussian avatars and 360° generators are previews, not bases. Judge likeness on the mesh under MatCap, never on a textured avatar.
2. **Capture as for multi-view fitting:** 7–9 neutral photos at 1.5–2 m with one lens, a scale object, and expressions shot separately (`reconstruction/multi-view-fitting.md`). Few-shot neural methods tolerate worse input, but they hide the error rather than remove it.
3. **Recommended 2025–26 pipeline.**
   1. MediaPipe or Sapiens landmarks per photo.
   2. Own PnP plus bundle adjustment. Use VGGT or MASt3R only when profiles defeat the landmark detector.
   3. MICA median β as initialisation.
   4. Joint FLAME or template fit with silhouettes.
   5. Optional Pixel3DMM UV and normal maps per photo as dense terms.
   6. Normal-based detail as a clamped shape key.
   7. Import with vertex order kept. Add Multires level 2 for likeness and level 3 for detail (Table 6).
4. **Check four things before sculpting.**
   - Leave one photo out and require its landmark reprojection error to stay under about 2 % of inter-ocular distance.
   - Profile silhouette error under about 1–1.5 mm at the nose tip and chin.
   - Left/right asymmetry kept, since priors symmetrise.
   - Ear height and inclination checked against the profile photo.
5. **Use generated views only for unseen regions.** If the back of the skull or the ears must come from FaceLift, DiffPortrait360 or Hunyuan3D, put that geometry on a separate shape key named "hallucinated". Sculpt over it, and never let it move the face.
6. **What goes wrong.**
   - Identity drift toward a dataset mean ("a relative of the subject").
   - Selfie perspective baked in by weak-perspective regressors.
   - Lighting baked into albedo and normals.
   - Gaussian floaters at hair and ears.
   - Generic or helmet-like hair.
   - Licence contamination: FLAME 2020, BFM and ArcFace-derived weights cannot ship in a commercial add-on.
7. **Gaussian-avatar reference.** Extract a surface (2D Gaussian Splatting or TSDF of rendered depths), mask it to skin, and import it as a non-rendering reference to spot systematic offsets.

## Algorithmic implications

- **Backend tiers.**
  - Tier 0, shippable: MediaPipe, numpy perspective bundle adjustment and a template or 3DMM prior.
  - Tier 1, user-installed research backends run out of process: MICA, SMIRK/TEASER, Pixel3DMM, VGGT/MASt3R, Sapiens.
  - Tier 2, preview only: avatar models, diffusion and LRMs.

  Each observation carries `{source, version, licence, measured|generated}`. 🧠 (design)
- **Weight by provenance, not by visual quality.** Generated views get **0 weight on identity β and on face vertices**. For ears and the occiput, use only a silhouette term at ≤0.1 of the weight a real view gets. 🧠 (design default)
- **Dense UV correspondences (Pixel3DMM-style)**, design defaults to tune on scans 🧠:
  - σ ≈ 2–3 % of inter-ocular distance (IOD) per sample.
  - Subsample at a stride of 8 px and use a Cauchy or Huber loss.
  - Drop pixels within 5 px of the silhouette or with normals more than 70° from the view ray.
  - Weight the whole term so that it equals, at most, the summed landmark term.
- **Face-specific normals.** Use a von Mises–Fisher κ for a mean error of about 12–15° (κ = π/2m² ≈ 23–36), not the 20° default used for scene models (`reconstruction/monocular-depth-normals.md`). 🧠
- **Camera initialisers** 🧠 (design defaults). Use VGGT or MASt3R poses only to initialise.
  - Accept them only if bundle adjustment converges to under 2 % IOD.
  - As a soft prior, use rotation σ ≈ 3–5° and focal σ_log ≈ 0.15–0.2.
  - Never take scale from them.
- **Identity priors.** Keep sibling defaults (MICA β σ floor ≈ 0.3, residual scale ≈ 1.4 mm); tighten to 1.2 mm only if Pixel3DMM's gain is confirmed. 🧠
- **Expression gating.** Treat a photo as neutral when every MediaPipe blendshape is ≤0.2–0.3 *and* SMIRK/TEASER ‖ψ‖ is under about 1 (in FLAME σ units). Otherwise fit ψ for that view and zero it at hand-off. 🧠 (design default)
- **Evaluation harness** 🧠:
  - NoW-style median, mean and std in mm, in both similarity and metric modes.
  - REALY-style per-region errors.
  - Leave-one-view-out reprojection.
  - ArcFace cosine between render and photo as a *monitor*, never a loss, because optimising it makes adversarial likeness.

## Disagreements, myths & open questions

- **Myth: "photoreal avatar = accurate head."** Photometric metrics reward texture; geometry can be centimetres off at the occiput and still score well. ↔️
- **Myth: "one photo is enough now."** Diffusion and LRM priors fill depth and unseen sides from population statistics. Distinctive profiles regress toward the mean. ↔️
- **"Dense prediction beats regression."** Pixel3DMM's gains are measured on its own NeRSemble benchmark. Independent NoW or REALY confirmation is not known to me. ↔️
- **"Geometry foundation models solve cameras."** They do for scenes; for 5–9 face crops with expression changes, landmark bundle adjustment may still win. No face benchmark exists. ↔️
- **Identity embeddings as 3D supervision.** Arc2Face/Arc2Avatar and MICA rely on ArcFace features, which are partly invariant to the shape cues sculptors care about (nose depth, chin projection). ↔️
- **Licences are moving targets:** (FLAME 2023 Open is now confirmed CC BY 4.0 [1]); VGGT commercial weights, LAM and the MetaHuman outside-Unreal terms (lib: community/awesome-lists-resources.md). ↔️
- **Open questions.**
  - The best 2026 (CVPR, SIGGRAPH, ECCV) few-shot head method.
  - Any millimetre benchmark for Gaussian or diffusion avatars.
  - Code and licence status for SHeaP, Avat3r and KaoLRM.
  - Whether FLAME-topology output from Pixel3DMM is more accurate than multi-view landmark fits with 5–9 real photos.
