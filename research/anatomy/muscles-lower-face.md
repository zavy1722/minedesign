# Muscles of the lower face, lips and chin

> The lower face is moved by thin, interwoven sheets rather than discrete bellies: orbicularis oris (interlacing quadrants, not a true sphincter), the superficial-lateral depressor anguli oris (DAO), the deeper-medial depressor labii inferioris (DLI), the deep paired mentalis and the platysma, all tied into the modiolus beside each mouth corner. For likeness they matter as surface signatures and as contaminants of a "neutral" photo: sulcus, marionette fold, chin dimpling and commissure height all change with tone. The lip–chin profile itself is set mostly by teeth, mandible and soft-tissue thickness, so a fitter must separate skeletal identity from muscle activation. Sourced numbers are few and mostly single-source; the profile norms and soft-tissue depths offered as priors are textbook knowledge awaiting verification.
Evidence status: 12 ✅ / 50 📄 / 39 🧠 / 25 ↔️ — 🧠 items are queued for web verification.

## Key facts

**Orbicularis oris (OO)**
- Not a sphincter: four quadrants (upper/lower × left/right) interlace, with strata running in different directions [1][4][33][34] ✅ ([1] and [2] are one title in two PMC records, counted once).
- A smaller marginal part lies under the vermilion, a larger peripheral part outside it [3] 📄. The marginal part curls outward into the everted vermilion and its rolled border; the peripheral part forms the skin lip down to the mentolabial sulcus 🧠.
- Largely borrowed: peripheral fibres come from neighbouring muscles (mainly buccinator, per one source); the marginal part arises from the modiolus [3][5] 📄. The lower lip adds incisivus labii inferioris slips from the mandible, buccinator and "caninus" fibres, and deep modiolar fibres [3] 📄.
- Superficial fibres protrude (pout) the lips; deep and oblique fibres press them on the teeth [3] 📄. FACS separates them as AU18/22 vs AU23/24/28 (Table 5) 🧠. A biomechanical model links OO activation and jaw posture to lip shape [31] 📄.
- Buccal branch of CN VII; labial arteries run between muscle and mucosa [3] 📄. The lower lip also receives marginal mandibular fibres 🧠.

**Depressor anguli oris (DAO)**
- Triangular: base on the oblique line and mental tubercle, apex at the modiolus [6][35][39] ✅. The base spans roughly canine to first molar 🧠.
- Cadaveric insertion ≈ 10 mm lateral and 10 mm caudal to the oral commissure [35] 📄; width 14 ± 3 mm [11] 📄.
- Overlies the lateral DLI; lower fibres continue into platysma, and a variable transversus menti slip may join both DAOs under the chin 🧠. Extra attachments (skin, OO, risorius, levator anguli oris [6] vs continuity with platysma and cervical fascia [20]) ↔️, probably complementary.
- Main depressor of the commissure [14] 📄 (AU15 🧠). One paper calls DAO uniquely human [19] ↔️; chimpanzee dissections may describe a DAO-like muscle 🧠 (uncertain).

**Depressor labii inferioris (DLI)**
- Quadrilateral sheet from the oblique line between symphysis and mental foramen, deep and medial to DAO, to the lower lip [3][7][36] ✅; insertion into OO [7] vs lip skin and submucosa [3] ↔️.
- Moves the lower-lip midline down and back [13] 📄 and everts it to bare the lower incisors (AU16) 🧠. Width 20 ± 4 mm [11] 📄. DAO overlaps DLI in 12 % on ultrasound vs < 5 % in cadavers [27] ↔️. DLI palsy after chin augmentation is reported [56] 📄.

**Mentalis**
- Paired cone from the incisive fossa/alveolar bone below the incisor roots, descending to the chin dermis [8][21][36][38][39] ✅; bony wording differs ([38] vs [39]) ↔️ but is compatible.
- The only lower-lip elevator: raises and wrinkles the chin boss and raises and protrudes the lower lip [8][13] ✅. Dermal insertions make the "cobblestone"/peau-d'orange chin [21][41][44] ✅, which is why deep-only toxin may not stop dimpling [44] 📄.
- 5 mm lateral to the chin point: skin-to-muscle depth 6.7 ± 1.4 mm, thickness 4.0 ± 1.4 mm, bone gap 1.1 ± 1.0 mm [40] 📄 (snippet values). The sum (≈ 12 mm) matches the classic 10–12 mm soft-tissue chin thickness 🧠.
- **Mentalis strain:** incompetent lips (resting gap > ~4 mm, proclined incisors, long lower face) are sealed by mentalis, flattening the sulcus and dimpling the chin, often in "neutral" photos 🧠.

**Innervation**
- Textbook: DAO, DLI and mentalis via the marginal mandibular branch [8][36] ✅. A 45-patient synkinesis cohort classed 95.5 % of DLI branches "cervical", with mixed DAO/mentalis supply [12]; a 10-hemiface dissection (cited in [27]) found DLI solely marginal mandibular ↔️. Platysma is classically cervical-branch 🧠; [20][23] give its facial part to the marginal mandibular branch ↔️.

**Platysma**
- Thin sheet from upper chest and shoulder fascia. Anterior fibres interlace under the chin, intermediate fibres attach to the mandibular border, *pars labialis* crosses the mandible deep to DAO into lower-lip dermis and OO, *pars modiolaris* reaches the modiolus [20][23][46] ✅. It covers the lower third of masseter [46] 📄; posterior fibres cross the mandibular body, not the angle as the earlier draft said 🧠.
- Midline decussation varies (de Castro types) ↔️; classifications are arbitrary and thickness and ageing unstudied [24] 📄. Bands visible at rest signal age 🧠.

**Modiolus**
- Fibromuscular node just lateral to each commissure where 5–9 muscles meet: core OO, buccinator, zygomaticus major, levator anguli oris and DAO [15], plus risorius and platysma elsewhere [50] ✅ (count ↔️). Labial OO and zygomaticus major bundles interweave obliquely inside it [15] 📄; micro-CT models exist [28] 📄; it is better defined in humans than other primates [18] 📄.
- Its nature is disputed: node, cluster of convergence sites (fetal histology [16]), own ligament [17], or a band to the dermis classed as a "false ligament" [51] ↔️.
- Facial artery lateral to it in 4/5 cadavers, anterior in 1/5, never medial [52] 📄. It moves down and inward with age (ultrasound, 45 young vs 45 elderly Asian adults) [53] 📄.

**Folds, jowl, sulcus, chin**
- Marionette fold: upper part from DAO's skin insertion; DAO, platysma and mentalis pull contribute; the lower end is tied by the mandibular retaining ligament; bone loss, gravity and elastin loss add [3][9][10][22][54][59] ✅. Depressor pull deepens it with age [53][54][55] ✅. A validated photonumeric scale exists [10] 📄; "type IV" folds resist filler and toxin [26] 📄.
- Mandibular ligament: osteocutaneous anchor on the anterior mandible ([22]; Furnas) vs present only beneath platysma as the depressors' and platysma's combined muscular attachment [47] ↔️. The jowl is redundant skin and fat with no osteocutaneous anterior border [47] 📄; jowling reflects loose platysma adherence behind the ligament [49] 📄.
- Mentolabial sulcus (supramentale, Bʹ): the concavity where peripheral OO gives way to mentalis/DLI territory. Its depth follows lower-incisor inclination, lip eversion, chin projection and vertical face height more than muscle bulk; usually deeper and more acute in men 🧠.
- Chin cleft vs dimpling: a midline cleft present at rest is attributed to incomplete symphyseal fusion [25] or a gap between the paired mentalis bellies 🧠 ↔️; dynamic dimpling is mentalis. Its "dominant trait" label is disputed [25][57] ↔️. No population prevalence was found; 7.2 % is cleft chin among 500 Yoruba with cheek dimples [57] 📄.
- Chin ptosis ("witch's chin") is judged at rest and in animation; poor mentalis reattachment after surgery bunches or dimples chin skin [42] 📄.
- Resting tone shapes the neutral face: mentalis toxin lowered the chin point in 10/11 patients and reduced dimpling [41] 📄; DAO toxin plus filler cut median corner droop from −4° to −1° [45] 📄; both rest on small studies [45][59] ✅. Chronic DAO hypertonicity is a proposed lip-ageing factor [55] 📄.

## Applying this to photo→3D face sculpting in Blender

1. **Capture.** Frontal, 3/4 and true profiles from ≥ 1.5 m (≥ 85 mm equivalent); teeth lightly together, lips relaxed, plus a gently-closed set if they part at rest. Profiles are mandatory: sulcus depth and chin projection are nearly invisible frontally. Raking light reveals the sulcus and marionette fold, but shadow width is not depth 🧠.
2. **Gate the neutral.** Mentalis lifts the lower lip forward [13] and dimples the chin [21]: flattened sulcus plus textured chin means strain. DAO tone shows as low commissures and a sharp marionette crease. Identity persists across frames, activation varies; send activated frames to shape keys.
3. **Measure on the camera-matched profile.** Place Sn, Prn, Li, Bʹ, Pogʹ, Meʹ; compute sulcus depth, mentolabial angle, lip-to-E-line and Sn–Stms : Stmi–Meʹ (Table 3). These point-to-line measures survive head-pitch error; commissure droop against the horizontal does not, so level the head first. A > 2σ value is fine for likeness if consistent across views, once lens distance and pitch are ruled out.
4. **Sculpt order.** Bone/base (symphysis height, chin projection, mental tubercles squaring a male chin) → soft tissue (Table 4) → lower-lip masses (two lateral lobes with a shallow central dip for the upper-lip tubercle; the down-facing underside is the main shading cue) → sulcus → commissures → folds → skin. Face Sets per region (lower lip, chin pad, DAO, DLI, jowl) mask brushes and seed weight maps 🧠.
5. **Classify the chin groove.** Visible at rest, symmetric, relaxed: skeletal cleft [25], so it belongs in the base and every shape key. Only when the chin tenses: mentalis dimpling, an AU17 shape key.
6. **Marionette fold in two parts.** Upper segment from the commissure via DAO's skin insertion [3][9]; lower segment tied to the mandible by the ligament [22]. Crisper near the commissure, anchored near the mandibular border, jowl stopping at the anchor with the prejowl sulcus in front. Depth is age-dependent [10]: take it from the photo.
7. **Modiolus as a pole.** Concentric OO loops end in a pole at each corner, flowing out along DAO (inferolateral), zygomaticus major (superolateral) and platysma/buccinator (posterolateral) [15]. Corners sit roughly 1–2 cm behind the lip midline in profile 🧠 (uncertain; check the 3/4 view) and tuck under the upper lip.
8. **Regions and layers.** DAO band ~14 mm wide at the mandible, DLI ~20 mm [11], scaled to the subject; DLI medial with slight overlap [7][27]. Build OO as two keys, pout and press [3], not one "pucker".
9. **Shape keys.** Sculpt AU15/16/17/18/20/22/23/24/28 on the base mesh before Multires; Multires detail does not follow shape keys, so bake detail to displacement 🧠 (version-sensitive).
10. **Failures:** sulcus too deep from an everted lip or top light; marionette lines baked from a frown; mirrored commissures (DAO/mentalis supply is mixed [12]); real one-sided lower-lip weakness "fixed" [56]; platysma bands on the young; wide-angle lenses inflating chin and lips; beards (use priors, flag low confidence); undisclosed filler or toxin [41][45], so ask the subject.

## Algorithmic implications

- **Landmarks.** Keep `stomion`, `labrale_inferius`, `pogonion`, `menton`, `cheilion_L/R` (`face3d/synthetic.py`); add `supramentale`, `gnathion_soft` and derived `modiolus_L/R`. Bʹ has no detector index: take the profile-silhouette point farthest from the Li–Pogʹ chord.
- **Silhouette, not correspondence.** Detector chin and jaw contour points slide over the surface with view. Fit them as silhouette constraints (nearest contour vertex per view, the "landmark marching" of 3DDFA) 🧠. Indices to verify: MediaPipe 61/291 corners, 13/14 inner lips, 17 lower-lip edge, 152 chin; dlib-68 (1-based) 49/55, 58, 9 🧠.
- **Priors** (Gaussian, mm, adults; widen if sex or ancestry unknown; weight 🧠 and single-source terms low):
  - `mentolabial_depth` 4 ± 2, truncated [0, 10]; `lower_lip_to_SnPog` 2 ± 1.5; `lower_lip_to_Eline` −2 ± 2 (± 3 if ancestry unknown) ↔️; `mentolabial_angle` 125° ± 12° ↔️; `lip_chin_ratio` 0.50 ± 0.06; `interlabial_gap_rest` 2 ± 2, ≥ 0 🧠.
  - Soft tissue over the fitted skull: Li 13.5 ± 2.5, Bʹ 11 ± 2, Pogʹ 11 ± 2, Meʹ 7 ± 2.5, with sex and BMI covariates 🧠.
  - `modiolus_offset` from cheilion: lateral 12 ± 4, vertical −3 ± 5 (blends [35] with 🧠).
  - Region widths DLI 20 ± 4, DAO 14 ± 3 [11] scaled by ch–ch / 52 mm; layered models may use mentalis thickness 4.0 ± 1.4 and depth 6.7 ± 1.4 [40].
- **Neutral gate.** Score each frame on chin high-frequency energy versus cheek, sulcus depth, lower-lip height, per-side commissure height against the stomion line and marionette contrast; fit identity to a robust multi-frame median and down-weight frames > 2σ off.
- **Identity vs expression.** Give Table 5 AUs signed direction priors (mentalis: lower lip +y +z; DLI: −y −z plus eversion; DAO: commissure −y), a small-σ L2 prior on AU weights in neutral frames, and a lower-face identity basis roughly orthogonal to them, so identity cannot absorb mentalis strain.
- **Static vs dynamic chin.** `chin_cleft_depth` (bone layer) and `mentalis_dimple` (AU17) never share a parameter.
- **Warp stiffness.** For Laplacian/ARAP template warps use relative weights of roughly lips 0.3, chin pad 1.0, jowl 0.5, mandibular-ligament anchor 2.0, plus a crease allowance along the marionette curve (heuristics to tune 🧠). Expose `dao_platysma_coupling` ∈ [0, 1], since continuity is contested [6][20].
- **Asymmetry and age.** Fit left and right independently with a soft symmetry prior (σ ≈ 1–2 mm on commissure height); never symmetrize a large AU16 left/right difference, which may be nerve weakness [56]. Tie modiolus descent [53], fold depth [10][54] and soft-tissue thickness to an age parameter that data can override.

## Disagreements, myths & open questions

- **Myth: OO is a sphincter** [1][4][33][34]; a single radial "pucker" cannot separate pout from press.
- **Myth: marionette lines are "DAO lines".** Insertion, ligament, fat, bone and skin all contribute [9][22][47][54].
- **Myth: mouth corners align with the pupils.** Mouth width (~48–56 mm) is less than interpupillary distance (~60–65 mm), so corners usually sit under the medial iris 🧠 ↔️.
- **Myth: sulcus depth is a muscle trait.** It is mostly dental and skeletal, modulated by tone 🧠.
- **Cleft vs dimple:** McGill says chin dimples are unrelated to muscle [25], mentalis sources disagree [21]; they describe different things (static cleft vs dynamic dimpling). The cleft's cause and heritability [57] stay ↔️.
- **Contested anatomy:** DAO, DLI and mentalis attachments; modiolus nature [15][16][17][51] and muscle count [15][50]; mandibular ligament [22] vs [47]; DLI supply [12] vs [27][36]; platysma supply; DLI overlap [27]; toxin spread to DLI/OO as a real risk [44][58] vs rare [43]; mentalis dose should not track thickness [43]; DAO "uniquely human" [19].
- **Harvest corrections:** [1]/[2] share a title; McGill's URL is its Office for Science and Society; en.wikipedia.com URLs [21][57] are as found; [47]/[48] are probably one paper, as are [16] and the 2024 PubMed fetal record; [17] and [51] may be one 2025 study. Refuted so far: none.
- **Open:** sex- and ancestry-specific norms for modiolus offset, sulcus depth and mentolabial angle; perioral muscle and platysma thickness on MRI or ultrasound [24]; n and measurement site for [11]; full text of [12][35][40]; [10] grade definitions; [27] coordinates; [29][30] unopened; any open Blender FACS rig mapping these muscles to shape keys.
